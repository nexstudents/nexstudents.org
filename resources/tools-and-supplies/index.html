HomeschoolGrades

The gradebook and lesson planner we keep the actual school records in.

It is the system of record here, not a spreadsheet: lesson plans by day with steps a student ticks off, grades, attendance, GPA, quizzes and transcripts in one place. The part that matters most is the transcript. A homeschool year that was taught well but recorded badly becomes a problem years later, at exactly the point when it is too late to reconstruct.

The free plan covers one student with five subjects a year, which is a genuine free tier rather than a trial. Paid plans start around $20 a year for five students. We pay for it and we are not affiliated with them.
