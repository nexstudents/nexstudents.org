# NexStudents — Free Placement Assessment

A placement test that actually places. Free, online, scored the moment the student finishes.
No answer key to mark by hand, no sales call.

**Live:** https://nexstudents.github.io/nexstudents.org/

## Why

Every free placement test we could find is a PDF a parent grades by hand. Every auto-scored one is
behind a shop. For a parent who just needs to know where their student actually is, that is a bad
answer. This fills that gap.

## What it does

- One passage, 12 questions, six comprehension skills
- Scored instantly, with a **per-skill breakdown** rather than a single number
- Every missed question shown with what was chosen and what was correct
- Start at the level just finished: **85%+ climb a level · under 60% drop one · in between is the
  placement**

## What it does not do

**It does not give a percentile, and it will not pretend to.** Saying "your child reads at a 6.4
level" requires a tested reference population of thousands of students. That norming is the actual
product the paid tests sell. Inventing the number without it would be dishonest.

What you get instead is where the student holds up, where they stop, and which skills to teach —
measured against a published skill sequence and against their own earlier results. If you need a
normed score on paper for your state, a proctored standardized test is still the right tool.

## Status

Prototype. Level B (grade 6 reading) is built. Levels A and C are next, then the ladder extends to
grades 4 through 8, followed by maths.

Scoring currently happens in the browser, so the answer key is recoverable by anyone determined to
find it. Server-side scoring lands with the backend.
